# Evolve: From Biological Lock-In to Biological Choice

Evolve is a conceptual framework exploring biological autonomy for consenting adults. The project proposes a transition away from biological lock-in, outlining a future where individuals can safely restore, correct, or voluntarily enhance living biological form and function through personalized, non-surgical pathways.

The working white-paper draft remains in the project's research archive. See the [public research scope](../research/README.md) for current boundaries and publication status.

## The Problem: Biological Lock-In

Adults have limited control over biological characteristics inherited at birth or acquired through development, disease, injury, and aging. Existing solutions generally require acceptance, external aids and prosthetics, temporary cosmetic treatments, or invasive surgical reconstruction. Evolve addresses the absence of a broad, accessible pathway by which an adult can define a desired bodily outcome and have living tissue gradually move toward it.

## Core Concept: Biological Autonomy

Biological autonomy is defined as the ability of a consenting adult to intentionally modify their body's living form toward a personally selected outcome. The framework categorizes these outcomes into three areas:

* **Restore:** Rebuilding living form or function lost through injury, burn damage, or disease.


* **Correct:** Addressing congenital or developmental conditions that limit independence or quality of life.


* **Enhance:** Making voluntary changes to healthy features for aesthetic or personal preference.



## The Proposed Transformation System

Evolve envisions an outcome-selection and coordination platform rather than a surgical clinic. The user maps their current condition against a desired digital outcome, and the system translates that into an approved, professionally supervised transformation pathway.

**Design Objectives:**

* Non-surgical or meaningfully less invasive than conventional surgery.


* Personalized, gradual, and continually monitored.


* Biologically integrated with the person's own body.


* Reversible or stoppable where possible.


* Accessible across economic classes.



## Scope and Ethics

This project is strictly limited to consenting adults. It explicitly does not propose or support reproductive selection, embryo design, partner matching, or population ranking. The objective is to increase available choices, not to impose a preferred human form.

## Roadmap

Evolve is currently in the foundational phase. The immediate goal is to precisely define the problem, terminology, and system boundaries. A schematic DNA visualization prototype now supports software exploration; it does not establish biological feasibility. Further work will separately examine scientific feasibility, technical architecture, ethics, regulation, and finance. See the [current roadmap](../roadmap.md).
